# Reflection #4

Pod Members: **Esme, Frida, Melanie, Reyna**

## Reflection Questions

* Name at least one successful thing this week.

One major success this week was running successful demos with both our mentors and managers. Those sessions validated that our core product flows are working, helped confirm that our feature set is cohesive, and gave us confidence that the project is in a strong place at this stage. We were also proud of how clearly we could walk through the product during demo scenarios and show the value of what we built.

* What were some challenges you and/or your group faced this week?

Our main challenge this week was presentation practice and making sure our delivery felt polished and consistent. The product itself was in good shape, but translating all of our technical work into a clear, concise presentation took several iterations. We had to spend extra time tightening transitions, refining speaking points, and aligning how we tell the project story during the demo.

* Did you finish all of your tasks in your sprint plan for this week? If you did not finish all of the planned tasks, how would you prioritize the remaining tasks on your list?  (i.e over planned, did not know how to implement certain features, miscommunication from the team, had to pivot from original plans, etc.)

Yes, we finished all of the tasks we planned for this sprint, and we are very happy with the current state of the product. While there are always additional improvements we would like to add, our sprint goals were completed, and we intentionally shifted focus toward polishing our presentation and end-to-end demo readiness. Moving forward, any remaining enhancements will be prioritized as post-sprint improvements rather than blockers.

* Did the resources provided to you help prepare you in planning and executing your capstone project sprint this week? Be specific, what resources did you find particularly helpful or which tasks did you need more support on?

Yes, the resources continued to be helpful, especially structured sprint planning guidance and mentor feedback during demos. Having outside perspectives helped us identify where our explanation could be clearer and where our demo flow could be streamlined. This week, the most valuable support was around communication and presentation quality rather than deep technical implementation.

* Which features and user stories would you consider “at risk”? How will you change your plan if those items remain “at risk”?

At this point, most core features are no longer high-risk, but the biggest remaining risk is presentation clarity rather than implementation. If this remains at risk, our plan is to keep iterating on slide flow, tighten the live demo script, and continue practice runs so each team member can confidently explain both their feature area and the overall product narrative. We will prioritize communication polish to make sure the final delivery reflects the quality of the work we completed.
